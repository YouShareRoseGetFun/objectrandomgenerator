Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

The generator runs in your browser

The object list is a static file, and the shuffle happens on your own device. The objects you draw are never sent to us and we keep no record of them. There is no account, no sign-up and no login, so we hold no profile about you.

When you press Copy link to this draw, your settings — how many objects, which categories, which difficulty — are written into the page address. That address is shared only if you share it, and we do not log it.

What our hosting provider sees

Pages and files are delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes request information in order to serve and protect the site: IP addresses, browser and device details, the page requested, timestamps and error logs. This is ordinary server operation, and we do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not follow you to other websites. What it reports is aggregate: how many people opened a page, which page, which country the request came from, and which browser was used.

Advertising and cookies

The site is free and may be supported by advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings and at aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings — the generator works without them.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or California, you have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. In practice we hold no personal data about visitors beyond the hosting logs described above, which Cloudflare retains for a short period for security and abuse prevention. Write to the address on the contact page and we will answer.

Children

The site is used in classrooms, so it is deliberately plain: no accounts, no comments, no user-generated content and no file uploads. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Questions about anything on this page go to the address on the contact page.
